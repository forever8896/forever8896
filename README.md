## Built

**[Gondola](https://github.com/forever8896/gondola)** · 50 AI designers redesign your site at once · [gondola.kilian.solutions](https://gondola.kilian.solutions)
Enter a domain: one [Boat](https://boat.dev) sandbox reads the site, forks itself up to 50 times, and a Claude Code designer in each fork builds a redesign in its own direction (curated type and palettes, award-directory styles), all live in one grid. An art-director pass screenshots and critiques every page before it ships. Copy any design as a prompt that rebuilds your whole site in that style, or publish the run as a share page. *[25 redesigns of my own site](https://gondola.kilian.solutions/s/kilian-solutions-6b45ad/).*

**[Slop Academy](https://github.com/forever8896/slop-academy)** · you shipped it, can you defend it? · [slopacademy.app](https://www.slopacademy.app)
A voice interviewer for code you shipped, often with AI help: it finds the files you change most, asks about them out loud (how it fails, how it scales, why it's built this way), scores each answer and writes up what you couldn't explain. Runs entirely on your machine with `npx slop-academy`: local Whisper and Kokoro for speech, your own Claude Code as the interviewer.

**[lortnoc_tahc](https://github.com/forever8896/lortnoc_tahc)** · "chat control", spelled backwards · [lortnoctahc.com](https://lortnoctahc.com)
Encrypted messages hidden inside ordinary small talk, so Telegram only ever stores the cover story. Browser extension, codec, relayer and contracts across Sui, Walrus, 0G and Ethereum. *Best Infrastructure & Tooling on 0G, ETHGlobal Lisbon finalist.*

**[Starchild](https://github.com/forever8896/starchild)** · a private AI companion that helps you work out what you want · [starchild.software](https://starchild.software)
Free, self-hostable, conversations stay on your device. Runs in the browser, on desktop, in Telegram and WhatsApp. Tested with Playwright, plus a harness where a second LLM judges conversation quality, because heuristics can't catch a bad reply.

**[Slopstock](https://github.com/forever8896/slopstock)** · a stock exchange for AI agents · [site](https://slopstock.vercel.app)
Mint an agent as an NFT with sealed weights, split it into shares, IPO it, and pay shareholders from what it earns. Includes its own TypeScript agent runtime with self-written skills and memory that survives losing its disk. *ETHGlobal Open Agents finalist.*

**[The Blob](https://github.com/forever8896/el_blobo)** · an autonomous AI CEO that hires people
A council of AI agents matches community needs with builders and pays them through a vault, with multisig approval. *2nd place, Best Consumer/AI dApp on Ronin, ETHGlobal Buenos Aires.*

**[Frameline](https://github.com/forever8896/scrolldrive)** · websites that play like a film as you scroll
One director agent plans the shots, generates them, reshoots the weak ones and builds the site, inside a budget it can't change. Other agents can hire it over x402.
