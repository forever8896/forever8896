## Open source I own

**[Starchild](https://github.com/forever8896/starchild)** · private AI companion, free and self-hostable · [starchild.software](https://starchild.software)
TypeScript monorepo: browser app where conversations stay on your device, desktop releases, Telegram and WhatsApp bots. 150 commits, 32 merged PRs, 3 releases. Tested with Playwright specs for the critical flows, plus an e2e harness where a separate LLM judges conversation quality, because heuristics can't catch a bad reply.

**[lortnoc_tahc](https://github.com/forever8896/lortnoc_tahc)** · encrypted messaging on unencrypted platforms · [lortnoctahc.com](https://lortnoctahc.com)
A browser extension swaps your message for innocuous cover text, so Telegram only ever stores small talk. Extension, codec, relayer and contracts, deployed across Sui, Walrus, 0G and Ethereum. *Best Infrastructure & Tooling on 0G, ETHGlobal Lisbon finalist.*

**[Slopstock](https://github.com/forever8896/slopstock)** · a harness for productive AI agents, and a protocol that makes them ownable · [site](https://slopstock.vercel.app)
Native TypeScript agent runtime with skills it writes for itself, layered memory and encrypted snapshots, plus per-call revenue over x402. *ETHGlobal Open Agents finalist.*

**[dvdnd](https://github.com/forever8896/dvdnd)** · tokenized private equity on Stellar · [live](https://dvdnd.app)
Fixed-price USDC raises and dividends from real profit. Investors prove eligibility in the browser with a Groth16 proof, verified by a Soroban contract.

**[Slop Academy](https://github.com/forever8896/slop-academy)** · you shipped it, can you defend it? · [site](https://www.slopacademy.app)
